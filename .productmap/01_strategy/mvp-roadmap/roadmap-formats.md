# Roadmapping

**Competency:** Strategy

## Summary

Create and maintain product roadmaps that connect strategy to execution and communicate priorities to stakeholders. A roadmap is a communication tool as much as a planning tool — the right format depends on your audience and planning horizon.

## Key Concepts

### Now / Next / Later

The simplest and most flexible roadmap format. Avoids date commitments while communicating priority sequence clearly.

- **Now** — work actively in progress this sprint or cycle
- **Next** — committed work coming up soon (next 1–2 sprints or ~4–8 weeks)
- **Later** — planned but not yet scheduled; may shift based on learning

Best for: early-stage teams, high uncertainty environments, internal planning.

### Theme-Based Roadmap

Groups work under strategic themes or bets rather than individual features. Connects each item to a business outcome.

| Theme | Outcome | Now | Next | Later |
|-------|---------|-----|------|-------|
| [Fill in theme] | [Metric it moves] | [Work item] | [Work item] | [Work item] |

Best for: communicating strategy to leadership and external stakeholders.

### Outcome vs. Output Focus

- **Output roadmap:** lists features and deliverables ("build X, launch Y")
- **Outcome roadmap:** lists metrics and goals ("increase activation by 20%", "reduce churn")

Prefer outcome-focused roadmaps wherever possible. They leave room for discovery and signal confidence in goals rather than locking in solutions.

### Roadmap Communication

- **Weekly:** share sprint goals and blockers with the team
- **Monthly:** review progress against themes with leadership
- **Quarterly:** reset roadmap priorities at the start of each quarter

Treat the roadmap as a living document. Update it when context changes; don't wait for the next cycle.
