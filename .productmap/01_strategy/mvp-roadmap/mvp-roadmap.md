# MVP & Roadmap — Overview

> Phase 1 — Strategy

MVP and roadmap work translates strategy into a sequenced plan for what to build and when. It connects the product vision to near-term delivery through opportunity mapping, scope decisions, and roadmap formats that communicate direction to stakeholders.
