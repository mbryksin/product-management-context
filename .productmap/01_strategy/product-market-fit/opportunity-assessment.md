# Opportunity Assessment

**Competency:** Discovery

## Summary

Evaluate and compare opportunities (ideas, bets, initiatives) to decide where to invest discovery and delivery effort. An opportunity assessment forces explicit consideration of size, evidence, and strategic fit before committing resources.

## Key Concepts

### Size of Opportunity and Strategic Fit

- **Market size:** How many users or accounts does this affect? What is the revenue potential?
- **Strategic fit:** Does solving this move you toward your product vision and OKRs?
- **Timing:** Why is now the right time? What changes if you wait?

### Evidence and Confidence Levels

Rate each opportunity on the quality of evidence behind it:

| Confidence Level | What it means |
|-----------------|---------------|
| High | Validated with user research, data, or real paying intent |
| Medium | Directional signals from conversations or analytics |
| Low | Assumption or hypothesis — needs validation |

### Go / No-Go and Sequencing

Not every good opportunity should be pursued now. Sequencing depends on:

- Dependencies (does opportunity B require opportunity A first?)
- Capacity (team bandwidth and time to impact)
- Risk (what happens if this bet is wrong?)
