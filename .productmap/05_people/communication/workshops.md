# Workshops

**Competency:** Facilitation

## Summary

Design and run workshops that align participants, generate ideas, or make decisions in a structured way. A well-run workshop compresses weeks of async debate into hours of shared output.

## Key Concepts

### Goals, Agenda, and Timing

Every workshop needs:
- A **single clear goal** — what decision, alignment, or output will exist at the end that doesn't exist now?
- A **timed agenda** — each activity has a slot; the facilitator keeps time
- **Pre-work** — brief participants beforehand so workshop time is spent creating, not catching up

### Activities and Facilitation Techniques

| Technique | Best for | Duration |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Sticky note brainstorm (silent, then share) | Generating ideas without groupthink | 10–20 min |
| Dot voting | Narrowing options to a shortlist | 5–10 min |
| HMW reframing | Opening up a problem space | 15–20 min |
| 2×2 matrix (value vs. effort) | Prioritising or comparing options | 20–30 min |
| Role play / user journey walk-through | Building empathy for a user problem | 15–30 min |
| DACI or RACI mapping | Clarifying decision rights | 20–30 min |

### Capturing Outputs and Next Steps

- Assign a note-taker who captures decisions and action items in real time
- End every workshop with a "Who does What by When" list
- Share a written summary within 24 hours — include decisions made, open questions, and next steps

## Common Workshop Formats

### Design Sprint (5 days)

- Monday: Map the problem, set the target
- Tuesday: Sketch and ideate solutions
- Wednesday: Decide and storyboard
- Thursday: Build a realistic prototype
- Friday: Test with 5 real users

### Retrospective (1 hour)

- What went well? (5 min silent, 10 min share)
- What didn't go well? (5 min silent, 10 min share)
- What will we change next sprint? (15 min discussion, 5 min commit to one action)

### Discovery Workshop (2 hours)

- Problem framing: align on the user problem and evidence (30 min)
- HMW ideation: generate opportunity areas (20 min)
- Prioritisation: vote on top opportunities (15 min)
- Opportunity assessment: define next steps for top 2 (30 min)
- Wrap-up: decisions, owners, next steps (15 min)
