# Design & UX — Overview

> Phase 2 — Generation

Design and UX work translates user needs and product requirements into interfaces, interactions, and systems that are usable, consistent, and delightful. It spans from early brief and wireframe through to the design system that ensures quality at scale.

- Figma AI — design generation, auto-layout, and component suggestions
- Uizard — rapid wireframe and prototype creation from text or screenshots
- v0.dev — AI-generated React UI components from natural language
