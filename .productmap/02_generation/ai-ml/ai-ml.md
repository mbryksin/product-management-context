# AI & ML — Overview

> Phase 2 — Generation

AI and ML work covers the product management practices specific to building and shipping AI-powered features, from problem framing and data requirements through to responsible deployment and monitoring. It requires PMs to bridge user needs, model capabilities, and ethical constraints in a single coherent product vision.
