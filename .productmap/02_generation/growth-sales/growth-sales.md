# Growth & Sales — Overview

> Phase 2 — Generation

Growth and sales work identifies and systematically exploits the levers that drive user acquisition, activation, and revenue expansion. It combines quantitative modelling (growth loops, unit economics) with structured experimentation and sales process design to create repeatable, scalable growth.
