# Risk & Compliance — Overview

> Phase 4 — Delivery

Risk and compliance work identifies, prioritises, and mitigates the threats that could prevent a product from shipping safely or operating legally. It translates regulatory and security requirements into actionable checklists and registers that keep the team accountable without slowing delivery.
