# Stakeholder Map

**Competency:** Delivery

## Summary

Identify, align, and communicate with stakeholders so expectations are clear and decisions get support. A stakeholder map makes your communication strategy explicit and prevents critical voices from being overlooked.

## Key Concepts

### Stakeholder Mapping and Influence

Plot stakeholders on two axes: **interest** (how much they care about the outcome) and **influence** (how much power they have over the decision or outcome).

| | Low Influence | High Influence |
|---|---|---|
| **High Interest** | Keep informed | Manage closely — your most important stakeholders |
| **Low Interest** | Monitor | Keep satisfied — don't let them become blockers |

### Communication Plans and Updates

Different stakeholders need different information at different cadences. Tailor your updates:
- Executives: outcomes, risks, decisions needed — weekly or monthly
- Engineering: requirements, priorities, technical constraints — daily or weekly
- Customers: shipping news, roadmap previews — as needed

### Managing Conflict and Expectations

- Surface disagreements early rather than letting them simmer
- When stakeholders conflict, escalate to the right decision-maker with a clear framing of the trade-off
- Document all significant decisions and the reasoning behind them (see decision-log.md)
